Frequently Asked Questions

How does real-time budget syncing work?
Sync Budget allows you to host a budget and share a secure share code with another device or partner. Changes made on either device update instantly so both of you stay completely in sync.
What should I do if my budget isn't syncing?
First, check that both devices have an active internet connection. Try pulling down to manually refresh the budget view or re-entering your share code. If issues persist, try restarting the app.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. Sync Budget is designed with privacy in mind and does not collect or sell your personal data. All shared sync communications are securely routed.
How do I reset or create a new share code?
You can manage your hosting status and share codes directly from your budget settings menu inside the app.

Privacy Policy for Sync Budget

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

At Sync Budget, we believe that your financial information is intensely personal and belongs strictly to you. We are committed to protecting your privacy by ensuring that we do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal data or financial budgets.

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Sync Budget application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We operate on a strict zero-data-collection model. When you use Sync Budget:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, account passwords, or any identifiers.
  • No Financial Data: Your budget numbers, expense categories, transaction amounts, and notes remain entirely on your device. We never transmit or store your financial records on our servers.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising identifiers, or cookies to track your behavior across apps or the web.

2. How Budget Syncing Works (Host Share Code)

Sync Budget allows you to sync your budget data with another person or device using a Host Share Code. Here is how this process works while keeping your data private:

  • Ephemeral Connection: When a host generates a share code, it creates a temporary, secure link between devices.
  • Direct Transfer / Minimal Relay: The shared data is transmitted securely (using encryption in transit) solely for the purpose of synchronizing budgets between the participating devices.
  • No Permanent Storage: Once the sync is complete, your budget data is not retained on any central database or cloud server. The share code expires quickly after use.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Local-First Architecture: All your financial data is stored locally on your device's secure internal storage.
  • User Responsibility: Because data is stored locally and synced peer-to-peer via share codes, you maintain full control over who you share your code with. Please keep your share codes confidential.

4. Third-Party Services

Sync Budget does not integrate with third-party advertising networks, marketing platforms, or data brokers. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to outside parties because we do not possess any of your data in the first place.

5. Children’s Privacy

Sync Budget does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Because the app does not collect personal data from any users, it is safe for users of all age groups.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to enforce clarity or adapt to new app features. Any updates will be posted directly within this policy, and the "Effective Date" at the top will be revised accordingly.
